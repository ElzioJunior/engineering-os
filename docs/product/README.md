# Product Documentation

Keep current, implementation-relevant product intent here. Suggested documents:

- `product-vision.md` — users, problem, value, scope, and non-goals.
- `business-flow.md` — main business flow and human approval points.
- `user-journeys.md` — actor goals and important journeys.
- `domain-concepts.md` — shared language and semantic distinctions.
- `delivery-scope.md` — current release/MVP boundary and deferred work.
- `risks-and-evolution.md` — known risks and expected product evolution.

When a durable product decision changes, create a BDR. Do not use mutable
product prose to hide decision history.
